# Investor update

1. Collect 3-5 metrics with previous values; ask for missing ones, never invent numbers.
2. Lead with the single biggest win. Be honest about challenges.
3. Make asks specific ("intro to X", not "help").
4. Call `investor_update`. The draft is saved locally and never sent.
